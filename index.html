Ongoing regimens

Supplements and medication are confirmed once a day and then apply to every reading that day. Anything else stays switched on until you turn it off. One per line.

Reminders

A nudge twice a day. Consistency is what makes every analysis in this app mean anything — a fortnight of near-daily readings is worth more than three months of occasional ones.

Reliable on Android. On iPhone, notifications only work once the app is added to the home screen and even then they can be delayed. The app also nudges you on screen whenever you open it, which needs no permission at all.

Assistant

Off by default. Switching it on sends a summary of your logged data to Anthropic's API so it can answer questions about your own numbers. Nothing else in this app ever leaves the device.
